    FIELD MANUAL / controls & advanced systems
    When systems arrive
    Your first Full/Short runs introduce systems a few at a time: run 2 adds Signal Hunt and boss routes, run 3 adds Wardens, specializations and quests, run 4 adds mutations and the Nemesis. Daily, Story, Siege and Training always have everything.
    Specialization
    At level 5 in Full/Short runs, choose Gunslinger, Codebreaker or Phantom. Each has a benefit and a tradeoff for this run only. Three optional quests then offer repair, cache or code-kit rewards. Progress is shown in the HUD; Being hit resets the clean-survival quest, without other penalties.
    Mutations
    Full/Short runs offer one experiment around 95 seconds. Choose 1–3 or press 4 to keep your build. Each mutation has a tradeoff and lasts only this run.
    Nemesis
    A rival scouts you after a 3-second warning, then retreats. It returns with 35% resistance to your top weapon. Defeat it once to choose a signature ability, fragments, or repair plus a cache. It withdraws during boss fights.
    Routes
    In Full and Short runs, each boss grants a permanent run ability and pauses the world for a route choice. Use 1–3 or tap a route. Route tradeoffs last until your next choice; boss abilities stack for the run.
    Wardens
    Blue shields absorb 55% of incoming damage for 3 seconds. Amber means an exposed 1.5-second rush: dodge and attack.
    Overload
    Near a relay, press G or tap Overload to commit the current three-relay chain: an elite pursues you, holds take 3 seconds, and relay timers shorten. Finish all three for two caches instead of one.
    Signal hunt
    In full and short runs, follow the cyan marker to a relay. Stay inside its ring for 2 seconds. Recover 3 before their timers expire to earn a Cache and repair 15% integrity. Optional: ignore them when survival comes first.
    Move
    WASD or arrow keys. Hold the mouse to walk toward it. On a phone, drag anywhere.
    Upgrade
    Click a card, or press 1, 2 or 3. If you've bought them in the Kernel: R rerolls, B banishes, X skips.
    Powers
    Q or Space fires slot 1, E fires slot 2. On a phone, tap the round buttons.
    Build
    Four weapons and four systems per run. Choose what to give up.
    Evolve
    Max a weapon, own its partner system, then open a Cache dropped by elites and bosses. Two evolutions per run, so pick them well; a third after you break the Mirror, or always with the Kernel's Third Signature.
    Pause
    Esc or P
    Reflex
    Shots that pass close to you charge a ring around your ship. When it's full, time slows to 30% just before a hit lands (or press V). You keep full speed.
    Overdrive
    Keep moving at full speed for 10 s: +25% speed and damage until you stop or get hit.
    Operator
    The round ◬ button (F) charges over time and faster as you collect shards. Call for a supply drop, temporary walls, a trace, or an Override strike on the toughest enemies.
    Exits
    After each boss an exit opens for 20 s: stand in it to jack out early with +25% fragments, or stay for more. After the Root Process, jack out for +50%.
    Daily
    One fixed run per day for everyone: same map, same rules, its own leaderboard.
    Anomalies
    Violet zones where the rules break: Stasis slows everything inside, Amplify makes your hits land harder, Drift pulls shards to its center at double value.
    Allies
    Keep an elite within reach for 5 s to convert it (up to 2). Wake a sleeping program in its pod and walk it to the exit: it stays and fights. A rogue program offers one trade when you stand next to it.
    Events
    Between bosses the Relay sets short objectives: purge fast, hold a beacon, or grab shards during a data storm. Success drops a Cache.
    Firewalls
    Grey blocks stop you and most enemies, and soak enemy shots. Your weapons pass through. Use them as cover.
    Enemies
    White line: a Lancer is about to charge, so step sideways. Red line: a Watcher is about to fire. Red pools hurt while you stand in them.
    Threat
    A small safety net: if nothing reaches you for a while, new enemies get up to 50% sturdier. Taking damage eases it off.
    Clarity
    Fades your own effects and zooms out so enemies stay visible. On by default on phones.

    Aftermarket / fragment exchange

    Cosmetics and archive ranks grant no combat stats. Contracts are optional challenges; prices are fixed.
